# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** [TODAY'S DATE]

This Privacy Policy applies to the **TrueTouch** mobile application (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). The App is intended for young children and toddlers as an educational and sensory experience.

## 1. Information Collection

We want to be perfectly clear: **TrueTouch does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information about you or your children.**

- **No User Accounts:** You are not required to create an account or provide any personal data to use the App.
- **No Data Tracking:** We do not use analytic scripts to track children's interactions. All progress is saved locally on your device.
- **No Location Data:** We do not collect or monitor your precise geolocation.

## 2. In-App Ads and Purchases

TrueTouch does not contain any third-party advertising or non-parental in-app purchases. The parent-only settings area may be protected by a parental gate (e.g., "Press 123 to enter").

## 3. Permissions

The App may request certain device permissions for local functionality only (such as vibration for haptic feedback or audio for sounds). These permissions do not send any personal information off your device.

## 4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the new policy on this page.
